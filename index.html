FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

A landing page takes 3–5 days, a business website 1–3 weeks, and an e-commerce store or custom system 3–8 weeks depending on scope. We agree on a clear timeline before we start.

Do you build in both Arabic and English?

Yes. Bilingual Arabic + English with proper right-to-left (RTL) layouts is standard on most projects, so you reach the whole Kuwait market.

Can I update the website myself afterwards?

Absolutely. We integrate an easy admin panel / CMS and train you to add products, edit content and manage orders without any coding.

Do you handle payments like KNET?

Yes — we integrate KNET, MyFatoorah and card payments so your customers can pay securely online.

What about hosting and the domain?

We can fully manage hosting, your domain, business email and SSL, or deploy to your own accounts — whichever you prefer.

Are the prices fixed?

The prices shown are real starting points for the Kuwait market. Your final quote depends on the exact features you need — and the consultation is always free.
